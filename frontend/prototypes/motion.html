How Tech Katta moves.

Twelve animations for the revamp, each live. Motion has one job here: show what changed, where it came from and what to do next. Every one uses three speeds and three curves, and turns off when the visitor's device asks for reduced motion.

Rules
  • Motion explains: it shows what changed, where something came from, or that an action worked.
  • Only transform and opacity animate, so it stays smooth on cheap phones.
  • The biggest motion is saved for the biggest moments: the first landing view, Submit, Mark complete.
  • Nothing you are waiting for is slowed down; content never waits for an animation.
Off switches
  • With "reduce motion" on, the device gets the end state at once: no drift, no burst, no count-up.
  • Loops (shapes, figures) pause when off-screen or when the tab is hidden.
  • Everything is visible at rest: no section starts invisible waiting for a scroll.
