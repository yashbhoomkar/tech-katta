How Tech Katta moves.

Twenty-five animations for the revamp, all live, in six groups. Motion has one job here: to show what changed, where it came from and what to do next. Every one uses three speeds and three curves. Slow everything down with the speed control to judge it frame by frame, or preview what visitors who asked for reduced motion will see.

Tokens

Three curves and three durations. A designer or engineer picks one of each; nothing else is allowed.

Arrive and respond · ease-out
cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1). The default: things entering, hover, press, open. Fast start, so it feels instant.
Celebrate · spring
cubic-bezier(.34,1.36,.64,1). Only for rare, happy moments: a tick, a star, the streak, a dropped part. Never on hover or press.
Travel · ease-in-out
cubic-bezier(.77,0,.175,1). Moving between two places on screen: a request along a wire, the tour spotlight. Sheets use the iOS drawer curve (.32,.72,0,1).
Fast · 140 ms
Colour and underline changes, press. You should not notice it.
Base · 240 ms
Hover lift, open and close, page change. Felt, not watched.
Slow · 520 ms
Arrivals on first load. Never on something the visitor is waiting for.

Reviewed against Emil Kowalski's animation standards
  • Source: the review-animations and choreograph skills in github.com/emilkowalski/skills (MIT), by the author of animations.dev.
  • Spring bounce now only for rare, happy moments (tick, stars, streak, a dropped part); hover, press and open use a strong ease-out.
  • Hover kept small (2–3 px) and only for mouse pointers; press is scale 0.97 at 160 ms everywhere.
  • Staggers no longer uniform: delays shrink and follow importance (headline 0/110/190 ms, cards 0/40/60/110 ms).
  • Bars, the tab underline and the travelling request move with transform, not width or left.
  • Sheets use the iOS drawer curve with no bounce; the Saved note uses Sonner's 400 ms ease and can be interrupted.
  • Nothing appears from a tiny scale: 0.9 for UI, 0.6 at most for celebrations; icon swaps hide the overlap with a 2 px blur.
  • Page changes, which happen often, are a 160 ms fade with no movement.
  • Reduced motion is gentler, not empty: movement and loops stop, colour and opacity still fade.
Rules
  • Motion explains: it shows what changed, where something came from, or that an action worked.
  • Only transform, opacity and colour animate, so it stays smooth on cheap phones.
  • The biggest motion is for the biggest moments: the first landing view, Submit, Mark complete.
  • Nothing you are waiting for is slowed down; content never waits for an animation.
  • Feedback starts within 100 ms of a tap, even when the answer takes longer.
Off switches
  • With "reduce motion" on, visitors get the end state at once: no drift, burst, shake or count-up. Try the switch above.
  • Loops (shapes, figures, traffic dots) pause when off screen or when the tab is hidden.
  • Everything is visible at rest: no section starts invisible waiting for a scroll.
