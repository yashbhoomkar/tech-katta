Caching: when to add a cache, and how to explain it

Yash Bhoomkar · Updated 9 Nov 2026 · 22 min read
▶ Watch Yash explain it · 6 min

Caching in an interview

Reading one user's profile from Postgres takes a few milliseconds. Do it 12,000 times a second and the database spends its day answering the same question. A cache in front keeps the answers in memory, so most reads never reach the database.

App servers12,000 reads/s
Redis95% hits · 0.2 ms
Postgres600 reads/s
Fig. 1 · Step 1. 12,000 reads a second arrive at the app servers.

Say this in the interview

"Reads outnumber writes about 100 to 1, so I'll put Redis in front with cache-aside and a 10-minute TTL."

Where to cache

On the device, at the edge, in a shared cache like Redis, or inside the server process. Each one is closer to the reader and harder to keep fresh than the one before.
