Dark mode, round 2

The light theme stays as it is. Round 1 picked A, but it felt dull: the pastels are low in colour, look grey on black, and nothing else on the page has colour. Round 2 adds two vivid answers. Each column is the same set of pieces from the site, coloured by that palette. Open any of them on the full site to judge it on every screen.

How to judge

  • A · Pastels on ink (round 1's pick): neutral black, the light theme's pastels dimmed, dark text on colour. Felt dull.
  • F · Vivid: the same idea with properly saturated colours (sky, orange, violet, mint, coral, yellow) on a slightly warm graphite. Closest to the light theme, with energy.
  • D · Colour as light: dark cards; each section's colour glows in a corner and lights its title, a top edge and the progress bar. Orange stays orange because it is light on dark, never dark paint (dark orange turns brown, which is what made the current dark muddy). Calmest at night, most premium.
  • Now: the current dark, for reference.

Look at it at night on your phone too: that is where dark mode lives. Every text pair in A, F and D keeps at least 4.5 : 1 contrast.
