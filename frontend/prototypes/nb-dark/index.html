Neo-brutalism, dark

The current dark felt wrong: neo-brutalism is ink on paper, and inverted it turns into neon on black. These are three answers to that, next to the current one. Each column shows the same pieces. Open any palette on the whole prototype with the link under it, or switch palettes there with the small "Dark" control.

How to judge

  • Now: cream outlines on warm black, calm pastel blocks. What you found unappealing: the blocks jump too far off the page and the cream lines read as a wireframe.
  • B · Chalkboard: a slate-green classroom blackboard. Chalk outlines, shadows darker than the page, section colours as "coloured chalk" (titles, underlines, bars). Only the hero cards and section headers keep a fill, a little dimmed.
  • D · Inverted blocks: dark cards with a thick coloured outline and a darker coloured shadow. Colour is carried by lines, so the bright area is smallest and the marigold keys stand out most.
  • E · Dim paper: lamp-lit rather than dark: a mid brown-grey page, black ink outlines and shadows (physically right), the calm pastels with dark text. Brightest of the four.

Look at it at night on your phone too. Contrast checked: body text at least 4.5 : 1 in every column; chalk outlines 8.4 : 1 (B), coloured lines about 10 : 1 (D), black ink on the dim cards about 2.7 : 1 but 3 px thick (E).
